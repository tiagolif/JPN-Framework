# JPN Creative Production Manifest v1

Status: INTERNAL / PRE-PUBLICATION

## Purpose

Turn the approved visual briefs into a reproducible production queue without publishing, buying media, inventing claims, or promoting release states.

## Shared visual system

Every candidate must preserve the JPN hierarchy: product name first, one clear problem/benefit statement, one visual proof or workflow, and one restrained CTA appropriate to an internal demo. Prefer whitespace, strong typographic hierarchy, consistent component spacing, and the established JPN visual language over decorative density.

Do not place price, discount, countdown, scarcity, revenue promise, guaranteed outcome, universal compatibility, customer logos, testimonials, or financial examples unless separately approved and evidenced.

## Production recipe

1. Read the corresponding product page and current tracked status before composing.
2. Reuse only claims already supported by repository documentation.
3. Build the hierarchy in this order: eyebrow/status, headline, supporting sentence, visual proof, restrained CTA/footer.
4. Keep each square candidate readable at phone size; avoid paragraph-length copy.
5. For screenshots or interface previews, use only tracked/fictitious demo data.
6. Export source/editable candidate plus PNG preview when the production environment supports it.
7. Run visual/editorial gates before changing any candidate status.
8. Keep all outputs internal until publication is separately authorized.

## Copy ceilings

Square card: headline up to 9 words; support up to 22 words; CTA up to 4 words.

Story: headline up to 10 words; support up to 28 words; no more than three supporting labels.

Hero: headline up to 12 words; support up to 32 words; one primary visual idea.

## Product-specific guardrails

### Método JPN
Show the three-layer transformation. Do not claim that the method guarantees correct AI answers.

### Prompt Builder
Show structure improvement, not task execution. Mobile QA remains a separate physical evidence gate.

### Prompt Pack
Represent templates as adaptable starting points, not guaranteed outcomes.

### JPN Business
Demonstrate playbook/application structure without implying autonomous business execution or guaranteed commercial performance.

### Gestão Fácil
Use fictitious/non-financial demo content. Do not claim universal Excel/LibreOffice/Google Sheets compatibility until the same XLSX passes the tracked physical QA. `REPOR` is an operational alert, not an automated purchasing action. Do not position the workbook as ERP, accounting, tax, or financial advice.

### Pro Kit
Always display `EM PREPARAÇÃO` while that is the tracked state. Do not imply availability, checkout, bundle price, or release readiness.

## Acceptance checklist

A candidate may advance to visual review only when: product/status are correct; copy matches tracked capabilities; no blocked claim appears; fictitious data is obvious where applicable; mobile legibility is plausible from the composition; source and preview naming follow the candidate ID; and no publication action has occurred.

## Naming

Use `JPN-CR-XX_<slug>_v1_source` for editable sources and `JPN-CR-XX_<slug>_v1_preview.png` for raster previews. Revisions increment the version rather than overwriting an approved candidate.

## Remaining evidence gates

This manifest does not satisfy physical or human QA. Prompt Builder mobile QA, Gestão Fácil cross-platform QA and GF3-QA-11..18, human PDF inspection, final freeze, hashes, release promotion, and publication authorization remain separate gates.
